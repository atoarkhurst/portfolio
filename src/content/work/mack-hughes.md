Mackenzie Hughes needed a site that felt established and professional without feeling dated. The work focused on organizing the firm’s practice areas, attorneys, and other content into a system that was easier to move through.

## The focus

Professional-services sites can get dense quickly. We used repeatable page patterns and a clear content hierarchy so visitors could start broadly and then get to the specific information they needed.

## What I worked on

- Responsive front-end implementation
- Flexible layouts for practice areas and team content
- Navigation behavior across screen sizes
- Visual consistency and launch-ready polish

This project helped me get better at translating an existing brand into a responsive website without losing its personality.
